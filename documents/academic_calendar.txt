# Academic Calendar | التقويم الأكاديمي

Source: Palestine Technical University – Kadoorie (PTUK)  
Academic Year: 2026–2027  
Source URL: https://www.ptuk.edu.ps/ar/academic-calendar.php

Note | ملاحظة:
Exam dates are based on the PTUK academic calendar. Practical final examinations are scheduled first, followed by examination periods generally used for theoretical finals. Where the official calendar uses only "Final Examinations," the theoretical classification reflects the usual university schedule rather than an explicitly stated classification.

December 13–17, 2026.

**Final Examinations – Generally Theoretical | الامتحانات النهائية – النظرية عادةً**  
December 20, 2026 – January 6, 2027, across four examination periods:
- Period 1: December 20–24, 2026 — Final Examinations | الامتحانات النهائية.
- Period 2: December 26–31, 2026 — Theoretical Final Examinations | الامتحانات النهائية النظرية.

- Period 4: January 5–6, 2027 — Final Examinations | الامتحانات النهائية.

The official calendar explicitly identifies December 26–31 as theoretical final examinations. The remaining periods follow the practical examination week and are generally used for theoretical finals.

The overall examination range includes breaks and holidays; exams are not necessarily held every day.
