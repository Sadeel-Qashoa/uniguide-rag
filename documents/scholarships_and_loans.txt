IMPORTANT | مهم:
Scholarship or exemption eligibility is not the same as an approved award. Terms, percentages, documentation, and deadlines depend on the applicable program and announcement. Dates in a dated announcement apply only to the stated semester and academic year. The rules below refer to PTUK's published 2026-2027 information.

=== 1. KEY TERMS | مصطلحات مهمة ===
Scholarship / منحة: Financial support toward university education, under specified eligibility conditions.
Tuition Exemption / إعفاء أو خصم: A percentage reduction of eligible tuition charges, depending on the relevant program.
Student Grant / مساعدة أو منحة طلابية: Assistance awarded after an application and eligibility review; not automatically guaranteed.
Student Loan / قرض طلابي: Financial assistance through a lending program; unlike a scholarship, it is subject to the loan fund's obligations and repayment rules.
Sibling Discount / خصم الأخوة: Exemption for eligible siblings concurrently registered at PTUK.
Students often ask: "في منح؟", "كيف أقدم على منحة؟", "في خصم أخوة؟", "كم نسبة الإعفاء؟", "بقدر آخذ قرض؟", "وين أقدم؟", "المنحة بتشمل الصيفي؟".

=== 2. UNIVERSITY SCHOLARSHIPS AND EXEMPTIONS, 2026-2027 | المنح والإعفاءات الجامعية ===

Children of higher-education ministry employees and children/spouses of qualifying university employees or retirees | أبناء العاملين في التعليم العالي والعاملين بالجامعة:
Rate: 100%.
Conditions: Required proof of employment; minimum cumulative GPA for continuation is 65% for bachelor's and 60% for diploma, with general exemption conditions.

Children of government education-sector or qualifying religious-school employees/retirees | أبناء العاملين في التربية والتعليم الحكومية ومدارس الأوقاف:
Rate: 50%.
Conditions: Recent salary slip and general exemption conditions; continuation GPA 65% bachelor's / 60% diploma.

Children or spouse of a martyr | أبناء أو زوجة الشهيد:
Rate: 100%.
Conditions: Relevant official supporting letter and applicable continuing eligibility, including published academic and family-status conditions.

Social assistance cases (government or UNRWA) | إعفاء الشؤون الاجتماعية:
Rate: 50%.
Conditions: Active qualifying social-assistance status documented by the relevant authority; continuation GPA 65% bachelor's / 60% diploma.

Sibling tuition exemption | خصم الأخوة:
Rate: 25% per qualifying sibling.
Conditions: Siblings must be enrolled and regularly registered at the university; general exemption conditions apply; continuation GPA 65% bachelor's / 60% diploma.

Qualifying current prisoners and qualifying family members | إعفاء الأسرى وعائلاتهم:
Rate: 100%.
Conditions: Official documentation confirming detention and required duration; eligibility depends on the detailed family and duration conditions published in the university policy.

Qualifying released prisoners | إعفاء الأسرى المحررين:
Rate: 100%.
Conditions: The official policy specifies minimum detention duration and qualifying family transfer circumstances. Supporting confirmation is required.

Children of Jerusalem scholarship | منحة أبناء القدس:
Rate: 100%.
Conditions: Graduation from the specifically listed licensed schools in Jerusalem's Old City, certified Tawjihi transcript, and cumulative GPA of at least 70%.

Top Tawjihi graduates scholarship | منحة أوائل الثانوية العامة:
Rate: 100%.
Conditions: Inclusion in the ministry-approved top-graduates list, certified Tawjihi transcript, and cumulative GPA of at least 85% after the first semester.

Disability-related scholarship | منحة ذوي الاحتياجات الخاصة:
Rate: 100%.
Conditions: Medical committee documentation establishing qualifying disability level of 50% or greater; continuation GPA 65% bachelor's / 60% diploma and general terms. The published yearly allocation is 10 cases at the main campus (Tulkarm), and 4 for each other branch; only Tulkarm cases are within this project's scope.

Full terms and supporting documents source:

=== 3. ENCOURAGEMENT AND MERIT TUITION EXEMPTIONS | إعفاءات التفوق والتشجيع ===

Bachelor's Tawjihi merit exemption | إعفاء تفوق الثانوية العامة للبكالوريوس:
Tawjihi Excellence Scholarship | منحة التفوق في الثانوية العامة

For students with a Tawjihi average of 99% or higher:
- 100% tuition exemption if the university cumulative GPA is 90% or higher.
- 75% tuition exemption if the university cumulative GPA is at least 85% but below 90%.

For students with a Tawjihi average of 96%–98.9%:
- 50% tuition exemption, provided the cumulative GPA is at least 85%.

For students with a Tawjihi average of 90%–95.9%:
- 25% tuition exemption, provided the cumulative GPA is at least 85%.
Diploma Tawjihi merit exemption | إعفاء تفوق الثانوية العامة للدبلوم:
- Tawjihi 98% or above: 100% exemption.
- Tawjihi 90% to 97.9%: 50% exemption.
- Continuing university cumulative GPA requirement stated by the page: at least 85%.
Supporting document: Certified Tawjihi transcript.

Agriculture bachelor's merit exemption | إعفاء تفوق الزراعة للبكالوريوس:
Rate: 75%.
Conditions: Tawjihi average of at least 70%, university cumulative GPA of at least 70%, and registration of at least 15 graded credit hours, plus supporting transcript.

Agriculture diploma merit exemption | إعفاء تفوق الزراعة للدبلوم:
Rate: 75%.
Conditions: University cumulative GPA of at least 60%, and registration of at least 15 graded credit hours, plus supporting transcript.

Mathematics, Physics, Chemistry and Biology merit exemption | إعفاء الرياضيات والفيزياء والكيمياء والأحياء:
- Tawjihi 95% or above: 75%.
- Tawjihi 90% to 94.9%: 50%.
- Tawjihi 80% to 89.9%: 30%.
- University cumulative GPA requirement: at least 70%.
- Certified Tawjihi transcript required.

Official university sports team achievement exemption | إعفاء التفوق الرياضي:
- First place at an official university sports participation: 75%.
- Second place: 50%.
- Applied to the semester following the achievement only.
- University cumulative GPA at least 70%.

Graduate master's merit exemptions | إعفاءات الماجستير التشجيعية:
The official 2026-2027 scholarships page also includes master's exemptions for specified programs (including computer science, software engineering and cybersecurity), plus programs with other rates and conditions. Refer to the official page for the exact major-specific percentage, undergraduate classification and semester/cumulative GPA conditions; do not apply bachelor's or diploma rates to master's programs.

=== 4. GENERAL SCHOLARSHIP CONDITIONS | الشروط العامة للمنح والإعفاءات ===
- A student eligible for more than one university scholarship or exemption receives the highest applicable one; university exemptions and scholarships cannot be combined.
- The published scholarship/exemption rules do NOT cover the summer semester | المنح والإعفاءات لا تشمل الفصل الصيفي.
- Exemptions are applicable to an eligible course only once. The policy excludes repeated courses, late-withdrawn courses, substitute courses, remedial/complementary courses, courses outside the study-plan load, certain pass/fail courses, and master's theses.
- Some parent-employment-based exemptions require the student to have been under 23 years of age when admitted.
- The policy describes limits for bridging students and eligibility across multiple degrees.
- A qualifying serious disciplinary warning may cancel scholarship/exemption benefits for the penalty period.
- Individual exemptions require the relevant paperwork and compliance with the university's approval process.

Source: https://ptuk.edu.ps/ar/new-students/scholarships.php

=== 7. STUDENT LOANS | القروض الطلابية ===

Student Loans Fund | صندوق إقراض طلبة مؤسسات التعليم العالي في فلسطين:
Loans are a separate financial assistance mechanism from nonrepayable scholarships and tuition exemptions.
Fund Website: https://www.iqrad.edu.ps/
Fund Electronic Services: https://eservices.iqrad.edu.ps/

General Application Procedure | طريقة طلب القرض:
1. Monitor official PTUK/Student Loans Fund loan application announcements for the relevant semester.
2. Log into the Fund's electronic services, or create a new account if required.
3. Complete or renew the semester loan application according to the current announcement.
4. Where required, submit supporting documentation to PTUK Student Affairs / Loans and Scholarships Department for verification. An online submission by itself may not complete the process.
5. If selected for a loan, check the Fund's result and complete the electronic acceptance and required promissory-note signing steps according to the published instructions.

Eligibility and award sizes are determined through the Fund's applicable conditions and review, not guaranteed by submission. The 2025-2026 announcements are examples of the procedure, NOT current 2026-2027 loan deadlines.

=== 9. UNIGUIDE ANSWERING GUIDANCE | قواعد الإجابة ===
- Distinguish clearly between scholarship/grant, tuition exemption and repayable loan.
- If a student asks "في منح؟" or "كيف أحصل على خصم؟", explain relevant available categories and identify the applicable academic year.
- State a precise scholarship percentage only with its eligibility conditions; do not imply that the student is automatically approved.
- Ask about degree (bachelor's/diploma), major, GPA, circumstances or relevant semester only if needed to narrow an ambiguous question.
- Do not reuse a dated announcement's submission window as a recurring annual date.
- If asked about summer scholarships, explain the published 2026-2027 rule that these exemptions do not cover the summer semester.
- Never claim that different PTUK university scholarships or exemptions can be stacked; the university policy says the highest applicable one is used.
- Do not ask students to send personal identity, income or medical documents into the chatbot. Direct them to the official portal.
- Respond in the student's language and provide the appropriate source URL.
