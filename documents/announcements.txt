## CURRENT-ANNOUNCEMENT RESPONSE RULES | قواعد التعامل مع الإعلانات الحالية

1. Treat dates and status as valid only as of the Last Checked date above; do not automatically assume an announcement is still open in the future.
2. Before saying applications are currently open, compare the present date with the deadline and, when possible, re-check the official announcement for changes or extensions.
3. If an announcement has passed its deadline, do not present it as a currently available opportunity. Update or remove its record from this current-only file.
4. Do not invent announcements, eligibility requirements, application dates, or working registration links.
5. If no applicable current announcement exists in the available sources, say: "لم أجد إعلانًا حاليًا متعلقًا بسؤالك ضمن إعلانات جامعة خضوري المتوفرة لدي." This does not mean the university has never published such an announcement.
6. Include the official announcement URL and the published date in answers where relevant.
7. Do not confuse annual scholarship regulations in scholarships_and_loans.txt with time-limited application announcements in this file.
