# PTUK Admissions, Registration and Academic Advising | القبول والتسجيل والإرشاد الأكاديمي

University: Palestine Technical University – Kadoorie (PTUK)  
Document Type: Student Knowledge Base  
Sources: Official University Procedures Manual, Admissions Pages, Frequently Asked Questions, Student Portal, and Academic Calendar.

**Important Note | ملاحظة مهمة**

This document summarizes general PTUK registration procedures and academic regulations. Requirements, dates, and exceptions may vary depending on the academic year, semester, degree program, and individual student circumstances.

Students should consult the latest official university announcements before making academic or financial decisions.

## 1. REGISTRATION AND ACADEMIC ADVISING | التسجيل والإرشاد الأكاديمي

**Registration Announcements | إعلانات التسجيل**

The Admissions and Registration Unit announces academic advising, tuition payment, and course registration dates through official university communication channels and the student portal.

Registration periods vary between semesters, and specific dates may differ between student cohorts.

**Academic Advising | الإرشاد الأكاديمي**

Academic advisors help students choose appropriate courses according to their study plans, academic standing, and completed credit hours.

Advising recommendations and notes may be displayed in the electronic student system.

**Course Registration | تسجيل المساقات**

Continuing students register for courses through the university's electronic student portal during officially announced registration periods.

Course registration is subject to academic regulations, course prerequisites, section availability, academic advising, and applicable financial requirements.

**Tuition Calculation and Payment | احتساب القسط ودفع الرسوم**

Students follow the official registration procedures to calculate and pay their semester tuition fees.

The required amount may depend on registered credit hours, tuition rates, applicable exemptions, scholarships, and other financial conditions.

The exact payment requirements and deadlines must be verified through the student portal and official university announcements.

## 3. REGULAR ADD AND DROP | السحب والإضافة العادية

**Regular Add and Drop Period | فترة السحب والإضافة العادية**

The regular Add and Drop Period takes place at the beginning of each academic semester.

During this period, students can add or drop registered courses according to university regulations and available registration services.

The duration of the Add and Drop Period varies depending on the semester and academic year. It may extend to approximately one or two weeks in some academic schedules, but students must always follow the exact dates published in the official academic calendar.

## 4. LATE COURSE WITHDRAWAL | السحب المتأخر من المساقات

**Late Course Withdrawal | السحب المتأخر من المساقات**

Late course withdrawal takes place after the regular Add and Drop Period has ended.

Students may withdraw from registered courses during the officially permitted late withdrawal period, subject to university regulations.

Unlike regular Add/Drop, late withdrawal does not normally allow students to replace a withdrawn course by adding another course through the same procedure.

**Late Withdrawal Dates – 2026–2027 | مواعيد السحب المتأخر**

- Last Day: April 29, 2027.

Summer Semester | الفصل الصيفي:

## 5. TUITION REFUNDS AND WITHDRAWAL | استرداد الرسوم والسحب

**Regular Course Add/Drop | السحب والإضافة العادية للمساقات**

According to student-reported practice, tuition fees associated with courses dropped during the regular Add and Drop Period are normally returned or credited to the student's university account.

**Late Course Withdrawal | السحب المتأخر من المساقات**

According to student-reported practice, tuition fees for courses withdrawn during the late withdrawal period are not refunded.

**Verification Note | ملاحظة التحقق**

These course-level tuition refund rules have not yet been confirmed from a current official PTUK financial regulation. They should not be presented as verified university policy until confirmed.

**Withdrawal from an Entire Semester | الانسحاب من الفصل الدراسي كاملًا**

Withdrawal from an entire semester is different from withdrawing from an individual course.

According to PTUK's published procedures:

- During the Add and Drop Week, an eligible semester-withdrawal request is treated as study deferment, with tuition fees retained according to the applicable procedure.
- After the Add and Drop Week and within the period specified in the procedure, semester withdrawal may result in loss of tuition fees.
- The withdrawn semester may count toward the maximum permitted study duration.
- Certain exceptions may require approval from the relevant university authorities.

Students should confirm the financial consequences of withdrawal with the Admissions and Registration Unit.

## 6. ACADEMIC COURSE LOAD | العبء الأكاديمي وعدد الساعات المسموح تسجيلها

**What Is Academic Course Load? | ما هو العبء الأكاديمي؟**

Academic course load means the total number of credit hours a student is allowed to register for during one academic semester.

Students commonly ask about academic course load using everyday expressions such as:

Arabic:
- كم ساعة بقدر أنزل؟
- كم ساعة مسموح أنزل؟
- كم أكثر إشي بقدر أنزل؟
- شو الحد الأعلى للساعات؟
- كم أقل عدد ساعات بقدر أسجل؟
- بقدر أنزل 21 ساعة؟
- كم ساعة مسموح بالصيفي؟
- بقدر أنزل مادة وحدة بالصيفي؟

English:
- How many credit hours can I register for?
- What is the maximum number of credit hours?
- What is the minimum number of credit hours?
- How many hours can I take this semester?
- How many credit hours can I take in summer?

These expressions refer to academic course-load limits.

**Regular Semesters | الفصل الأول والفصل الثاني**

According to the PTUK Frequently Asked Questions page, the standard course load is:

Minimum: 12 credit hours.  
Maximum: 19 credit hours.

The allowed number of hours may change depending on the student's academic standing and applicable exceptions.

**Academic Warning | الإنذار الأكاديمي**

According to the FAQ, students under academic warning may register for a maximum of 15 credit hours with the approval of the department head.

**Additional Credit Hours | الساعات الإضافية**

The FAQ describes situations in which students may register for up to 21 credit hours, including graduating students and students with a cumulative average of 80% or higher.

Other program-specific conditions may apply.

**Exceptional Minimum | الحد الأدنى الاستثنائي**

In exceptional circumstances, students may register for 9 credit hours with the dean's approval, according to the FAQ.

**Summer Semester | الفصل الصيفي**

According to the student-provided information, the regular summer semester course-load range is:

Minimum: 1 credit hour.  
Maximum: 10 credit hours.

This means students may register for as little as one credit hour during the summer semester, subject to course availability and applicable registration requirements.

**Graduating Students Exception | استثناء الطلبة الخريجين**

Graduating students may qualify for an exception allowing them to register for more than the regular summer maximum of 10 credit hours.

Such exceptions depend on the university's applicable regulations and required approvals. The permitted additional hours must be verified before providing an exact maximum.

**Summer Credit-Hour Verification | التحقق من ساعات الصيفي**

The 1–10 credit-hour range and the graduating-student exception were provided as student knowledge. They have not yet been fully verified against current official PTUK academic regulations.

## 7. STUDY DEFERMENT | تأجيل الدراسة

**Study Deferment | تأجيل الدراسة**

Study deferment allows eligible students to postpone their studies for an academic semester according to university regulations.

Students must submit their deferment requests through the officially designated procedure and within the permitted period.

**Deferment Conditions | شروط التأجيل**

According to the published university procedures:

- The student must not be academically dismissed.
- The student must not be in their first semester at the university or in their current academic major.
- The student must not have exhausted the permitted deferment period.
- The procedure specifies a maximum of four semesters of deferment for diploma and bachelor's students, whether consecutive or separate.

Study deferment requests are subject to university verification and approval.

Source:  
https://ptuk.edu.ps/procedures_manual_3/Dashboard.php?id=32&page=ProcedureDetails

## 8. WITHDRAWAL FROM AN ENTIRE SEMESTER | الانسحاب من الفصل الدراسي كاملًا

**Semester Withdrawal | الانسحاب من الفصل**

Semester withdrawal means withdrawing from an entire academic semester rather than dropping one or more individual courses.

According to university procedures, eligible students can submit a semester-withdrawal request through the designated university service.

**During Regular Add/Drop | خلال السحب والإضافة العادية**

An eligible semester-withdrawal request during the Add and Drop Week is treated as study deferment, with the financial treatment specified in university procedures.

**After Regular Add/Drop | بعد السحب والإضافة العادية**

The published procedure describes semester withdrawal after the Add and Drop Week and up to the end of the tenth week, subject to applicable regulations.

Withdrawal during this period may result in loss of semester tuition fees and may count toward the maximum permitted study duration.

Certain exceptions may require additional university approval.

## 10. UNIVERSITY WITHDRAWAL AND READMISSION | الانسحاب من الجامعة وإعادة الالتحاق

**University Withdrawal | الانسحاب من الجامعة**

University withdrawal means formally leaving the university, rather than withdrawing from a particular course or semester.

## 13. UNIGUIDE AI RESPONSE GUIDELINES | تعليمات إجابات المساعد

1. Answer registration questions using the available PTUK information and cite relevant official sources.

2. When a student asks about registration dates, Add/Drop dates, or withdrawal dates, specify the academic semester and year.

3. If the question is ambiguous and different semesters have different answers, ask which semester the student means.

4. Understand informal student expressions. For example, "كم ساعة بقدر أنزل؟" is a question about the permitted number of credit hours, not about downloading files or materials.

5. When asked about maximum or minimum credit hours without a semester, clarify whether the student means a regular semester or summer semester.

6. Explain special conditions when credit-hour limits depend on academic warning, GPA, graduating status, or university approval.

7. Do not state the summer 1–10 credit-hour range or the graduating-student exception as officially verified until an authoritative source is confirmed.

8. Clearly distinguish between regular Add/Drop, late course withdrawal, study deferment, semester withdrawal, and university withdrawal.

9. Do not present unverified course-level tuition refund rules as confirmed official policy.

10. When official university sources conflict, explain the discrepancy instead of choosing an unsupported answer.

11. If a specific student's registration date or eligibility cannot be determined from the available documents, state that the information is insufficient.

12. Never invent dates, tuition fees, credit-hour limits, approvals, or academic regulations.

13. Provide the relevant official source URL whenever possible.
