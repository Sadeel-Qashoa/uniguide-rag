IMPORTANT SCOPE NOTE | ملاحظة مهمة
Practical training, field training, clinical training, and cooperative/integrated education are not necessarily the same university activity. Requirements differ by academic program, course, and study plan. Do not use the hours, prerequisites, or grading rules from one program as a universal requirement for every student.
التدريب العملي والتدريب الميداني والتدريب السريري والتعليم التكاملي قد تكون مساقات أو أنظمة مختلفة. عدد الساعات والمتطلبات وآلية التقييم تختلف حسب التخصص والمساق والخطة الدراسية.

=== 1. WHAT IS PRACTICAL / FIELD TRAINING? | ما هو التدريب العملي أو الميداني؟ ===
Practical or field training allows students to apply academic knowledge in a real workplace, such as a company, factory, hospital, school, or other relevant institution, under the requirements of their program.
التدريب العملي أو الميداني هو تطبيق ما تعلمه الطالب في بيئة عمل فعلية، حسب طبيعة تخصصه وتعليمات مساق التدريب.
Student-language examples | أسئلة الطلاب:
- كم ساعة تدريب علي؟
- متى بقدر أسجل التدريب؟
- بقدر أتدرب بالصيفي؟
- كيف أعتمد التدريب؟
- بقدر أختار الشركة لحالي؟
- شو لازم أسلم بعد التدريب؟
- How many training hours do I need?
- When am I eligible for field training?
- What reports or forms are required?

=== 3. HOW TO REGISTER FOR TRAINING | كيف يسجل الطالب التدريب؟ ===
A student should check their specific study plan, the training-course prerequisite, the relevant department's instructions, and the student portal's course availability. Being eligible to enroll in a course does not automatically establish approval of a particular workplace.
على الطالب مراجعة خطته الدراسية، والمتطلب السابق لمساق التدريب، وتعليمات القسم، وتوفر المساق على البوابة. الموافقة على جهة تدريب معينة قد تحتاج تنسيقًا مع القسم أو المشرف.
Do not invent a universal number of credit hours needed before training: the published logistics example requires 80 completed credit hours, but that cannot be generalized to other majors.

=== 4. CHOOSING A TRAINING ORGANIZATION | اختيار جهة التدريب ===
Training is intended to take place in an organization relevant to the student's major. Published course descriptions refer to coordination with the academic department or college. Some integrated education programs use lists of partner organizations.
يجب أن تناسب جهة التدريب تخصص الطالب، مع مراعاة متطلبات القسم وموافقاته. لا تفترض أن الطالب يستطيع اعتماد أي شركة دون التنسيق المطلوب.
For a specific student's question about a chosen company, the exact approval procedure is not established in this knowledge file; the student should contact their department or academic training supervisor.

=== 5. TRAINING DOCUMENTS AND REPORTS | أوراق التدريب والتقارير ===
Document requirements are program-dependent. Examples of possible documents, especially for integrated/cooperative programs, include:
- Training letter | كتاب التدريب.
- Training contract | عقد التدريب.
- Training plan | الخطة التدريبية.
- Attendance sheet | كشف الدوام.
- Midterm or monthly training report | تقرير تدريب نصفي أو شهري.
- Workplace supervisor's final evaluation | تقييم المشرف الميداني.
- Student training program feedback | تقييم الطالب للبرنامج التدريبي.
Not every document on this list is required for every conventional field-training course.
Source for integrated/cooperative training: https://ptuk.edu.ps/procedures_manual_3/Dashboard.php?id=107&page=ProcedureDetails

=== 6. DURING THE TRAINING | أثناء التدريب ===
The student should follow approved training instructions, attend the training workplace as required, complete assigned practical tasks, and submit reports or attendance documentation required by the specific course.
يقوم الطالب بالالتزام بالدوام والتعليمات في مكان التدريب، وتنفيذ المهام، وتسليم التقارير المطلوبة لمساقه.
Some course descriptions explicitly require reports and meetings with the academic supervisor. Integrated education procedures additionally describe periodic academic supervision and possible workplace visits.

=== 7. EVALUATION AND APPROVAL | التقييم واعتماد التدريب ===
The assessment method must be taken from the specific course or academic program; there is no verified universal percentage distribution for all PTUK field-training courses in this file.
طريقة احتساب علامة التدريب تختلف بحسب المساق أو البرنامج؛ لا تعمم نسب تقييم التعليم التكاملي على التدريب العادي.
For integrated education courses covered by PTUK's published integrated-training supervision procedure, the evaluation distribution is:
- 50% academic supervisor | 50% المشرف الأكاديمي.
- 25% workplace supervisor | 25% المشرف الميداني.
- 25% joint evaluation based on the field visit | 25% تقييم مشترك بناءً على الزيارة الميدانية.
This 50/25/25 distribution applies to the specified integrated education procedure only, not automatically to all training courses.
Source: https://ptuk.edu.ps/procedures_manual_3/Dashboard.php?id=107&page=ProcedureDetails

The PTUK grade-entry procedure states that lecturers supervising field training submit field-training results to Admissions and Registration during the practical final-examination week for the first and second semesters, under the relevant training procedure. This is an administrative deadline for supervisors, not necessarily a student report deadline.
Source: https://ptuk.edu.ps/procedures_manual_3/Dashboard.php?id=114&page=ProcedureDetails

=== 8. SUMMER TRAINING, PAID TRAINING, AND OUTSIDE ORGANIZATIONS | الصيفي والأجر والتدريب الخارجي ===
Summer training | التدريب الصيفي:
Whether a student can complete or officially register training during summer depends on the program, academic course offering, and departmental approval. Some program descriptions schedule training between semesters, but this does not prove every major accepts summer training.

Paid or unpaid training | التدريب المدفوع أو غير المدفوع:
The collected official course and procedure descriptions do not establish a university-wide requirement that companies must pay students for practical training. Payment depends on the training arrangement and the host organization; do not promise a salary or stipend.

Training at a student-selected organization | التدريب في شركة من اختيار الطالب:
Departmental coordination and approval may be required. The student should verify suitability and paperwork before beginning the training.

=== 10. UNIGUIDE AI RESPONSE NOTES | ملاحظات الإجابة ===
- For questions about training requirements, ask for the student's major if missing.
